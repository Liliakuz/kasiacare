AARP Caregiving Resource Center

Practical tools, guides, and support for family caregivers — including how to talk to a doctor, manage medications, navigate long-term care options, and care for yourself while caring for someone else.

The leading voluntary health organization in Alzheimer's care, support, and research. Offers a 24/7 helpline, local support groups, care consultations, and extensive caregiver resources.

Support for cancer patients and the families who care for them — including a 24/7 helpline, transportation assistance, lodging near treatment centers, and caregiver guides.

Eldercare Locator

Meals on Wheels America

Connects older adults with local meal delivery programs that bring nutritious meals to the home daily or weekly. Also offers friendly visitor and wellness check-in services.
