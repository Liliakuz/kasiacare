# KasiaCare 💙

### Care Plan Software for Individuals, Families, Caregivers & Care Teams

---

> *"No one receiving care should feel unknown. No one providing care should feel unprepared."*
> — KasiaCare Mission Statement

---

## The Dream

KasiaCare began with one simple belief — that everyone deserves a care plan.

As we get older, face a disability, or navigate a health challenge, the people around us need to know what we need, when we need it, and how to help. Right now, millions of families are coordinating care through notebooks, text messages, and memory. Caregivers walk through the door with no idea what that person's day looks like. Critical information gets lost at every handoff.

KasiaCare is being built to change that — one care plan at a time.

---

## Who This Is For

- **Individuals** managing their own care — whether recovering, living with a disability, or simply wanting to stay on top of their health
- **Families** coordinating care for a loved one — knowing what was done, what is needed, and who is coming next
- **Caregivers & Personal Aids** who need to walk through the door ready — with a clear picture of the person's full day
- **Care Teams** — everyone on the same plan, in real time, wherever care happens

---

## What KasiaCare Does

KasiaCare helps people **track, report, and maintain** their care plan.

We do not recommend care, treatments, medications, or medical solutions. All care decisions are made by the caregiver, the person receiving care, their family, and or their medical team.

---

## The App

KasiaCare is currently in active development. A working demo is available at [kasiacare.com](https://kasiacare.com).

The demo includes:
- Seven care plan categories — Activities · Appointments · Meals · Med Reminders · Notes · Orders · Tasks
- Full care plan calendar
- Six food-based Med Reminder slots
- Today · This Week · This Month views
- Add items, check them off, view your care plan
