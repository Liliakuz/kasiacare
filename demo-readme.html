What Is the Demo?
The KasiaCare demo is a working care plan app. Sign in with your name and email to get started. You can add items, check them off, view your calendar, and explore all seven care plan categories. All data is temporary — it resets when you close the app. Nothing is saved or stored.
Best experience: Open this demo in Chrome or Samsung Browser on your phone for the best experience.
