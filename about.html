Our Story

Every line of code written in memory of Kasia.

Kasia in her favorite chair

KasiaCare is named in honor of Kasia — pronounced KAH-shah — a beloved mother who lived with a disability and was cared for by her family for many years. She had a radiant smile, a love for her daughters, and an unwavering spirit.

Her daughter Lilia was her primary caregiver for over thirty years. There was no shared care plan. No app that traveled with Kasia wherever she went — whether she was at the hospital, the doctor's office, or visiting family. Every handoff meant starting over.

Lilia's dream was simple — that "No caregiver should ever walk through the door unprepared, and no person receiving care should ever feel unknown." KasiaCare was built to make that dream a reality — taking shape one careful step at a time, with every line of code written in memory of her mama, Kasia.

Kasia passed away on February 7, 2023. KasiaCare is Lilia's way of making sure families, caregivers, and individuals have the simplest way of creating a basic care plan.
