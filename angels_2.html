What is the Angels Program?

KasiaCare Angels are our earliest supporters — people who believe in what we are building and want to help shape it before it launches to the world.

As an Angel you get early access to KasiaCare and the knowledge that your feedback is making the product better for every family that comes after you.

There is no cost to be an Angel. We ask only for your honest feedback and your care for this community.

"Uniting our actions and dreams to reflect what we want to help people achieve — in the most basic way — just to have a simple care plan."

— Lilia Kuzmicz, Founder

What Angels Do

One role — many ways to contribute

Every KasiaCare Angel is simply that — an Angel. There is no complicated structure, no required commitment, and no pressure. You show up however you can, and every bit of it matters.

Here are some of the ways Angels contribute — pick what feels right for you:

📋 Care Plan Support — Help new users set up and organize their care plan. Share your caregiving experience to make the product better for everyone.

📣 Social Media & Outreach — Share KasiaCare in caregiver communities, Facebook groups, and social media. Help families who need a care plan find us.

🔍 Software Testing & Feedback — Try the software, explore the features, and share honest feedback. No technical experience needed — just real-world eyes on the product.

What Angels Receive

Our commitment to you

🎉 Early Access

💬 Direct Line to Lilia
